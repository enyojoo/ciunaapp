Tone

Plain, calm, specific. Ciuna talks to people who may be new to the country, new to the language and careful with money. Say what happens, to which money, and when. Warm, not cute. Short words. One idea per sentence. Address the person as "you"; the product speaks as the system.

Sentence case

Titles, buttons, tabs and menu items use sentence case. Product names keep capitals: Ciuna, Ciuna Seller, Send, Experts.

DoAdd a new productPayout historyOnly the first word and proper names are capitalised.
Don'tAdd A New ProductPAYOUT HISTORYTitle Case and shouting read as a form, not a person.

Errors say what to fix

Name the field, say what is wrong and how to fix it. Never blame the person. Never show codes or stack text.

Money states: say what happens and when

Tell people where the money is. Calm statements, no exclamation marks, no celebration on a transaction.

Do Payment received. Mama T's Kitchen has your ₽1,250. Your payout of ₽12,400 will arrive by Tue, Sep 29. Refund of ₽700 sent. It can take up to 5 business days to appear. Held until the order is delivered, then released to the seller.
Don't Yay! You paid!!! Your money is on its way soon Transaction processed successfully Funds locked in escrow via YooKassa

No payment providers on customer or seller screens

Customers and sellers never see YooKassa, Bitbanker or any other provider name: not on screens, emails, push or errors. Office is the only place providers are named.

Dates and times

"Sep 24" in lists. "Sep 24, 2026 • 2:14 PM" in details. Cut-offs and bookings carry a time zone. Bookings show the viewer's zone and the expert's.

Many languages

  • Every string exists in en, ru, fr, es, zh, pt. Write the English so it translates: short sentences, no idioms, no puns, no jargon.
  • Never build a sentence from pieces. Use whole strings with placeholders: "{name} accepted your order".
  • Leave room for 40% more text. Russian and French run longest.
  • Names, addresses and business names stay in the script they were written in. Never transliterate someone's name.
  • Numbers, money and dates are formatted by the system, not typed into the string.
  • Same voice in every language: polite, direct, no slang. Use the formal "you" where the language has one (Russian "вы", French "vous").
