Rules

Use
  • Chips filter the list below them in place; no page reload.
  • Keep labels to one or two words; counts only when they help.
  • Put the most common choice first.
Don't
  • Don't use chips as navigation (use tabs) or as form choices (use radio).
  • Don't wrap chips to a second line on phones.
  • Don't show a chip with count 0 as active; dim it.
