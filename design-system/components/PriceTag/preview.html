Use

  • Symbol first, comma thousands, tabular figures.
  • Always show the price the customer pays, in the pay currency (RUB).
  • Pair the strike-through with a percent badge so the saving reads in words.
  • Say "From" or "/ hour" in muted text next to the number.

Don't

  • Use ₽0 for a quote or free item; write "Price on request" or "Free".
  • Colour the sale price orange or red.
  • Show cents on whole amounts in lists (₽850.00).
  • Show conversion rates or a second currency next to the price.
