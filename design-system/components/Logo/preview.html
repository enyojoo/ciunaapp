Clear space

xxxx
Keep a margin of x on every side, where x is the diameter of one circle in the mark (about 44% of the logo height). Nothing else (text, edges, other logos, photos) enters the margin.

Minimum sizes

Wordmark: 22px high (about 80px wide). The app header size.
Seller and Office sidebar: 28 to 36px.
Mark alone: 16px (favicon).
Mark in headers and avatars: 32px.

Primary · white logo

The orange logo only goes on paper, white or primary-tint. On photos, use a plain white or ink panel behind it. Never put the orange logo on primary: it vanishes.

App icon

The mark centred on a rounded tile (corner radius 22%). Mark fills about half the tile. Default is the logo orange mark on white; a dark tile is allowed for notifications and the lock screen. The tile is never orange.
