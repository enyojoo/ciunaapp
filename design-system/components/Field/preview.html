Rules

Use
  • Label above the control, left-aligned, sentence case.
  • Link label and control with for/id; connect hint and error with aria-describedby.
  • One short error that says what to fix. Summarise errors at the top only on long forms.
Don't
  • Don't mark required with a colour or an asterisk alone: write "Required".
  • Don't place labels inside the control or rely on the placeholder.
  • Don't show a hint and an error together; the error wins.
