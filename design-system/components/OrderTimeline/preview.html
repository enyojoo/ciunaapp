Use

  • Use the same stage words as StatusBadge: New, Paid, Accepted, Preparing, Ready, Shipped, Completed, Cancelled, Refunded.
  • Give every stage a time and who did it; upcoming stages say what has to happen.
  • Show the refund amount and how long it takes.
  • Put the meeting link in the booking's current stage.

Don't

  • Colour Cancelled or Refunded red; they are neutral.
  • Hide upcoming stages; people want to know what comes next.
  • Invent new stage names per store.
  • Use orange for a completed stage; completed is success green.
