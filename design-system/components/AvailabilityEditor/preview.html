Use

  • Show the time zone beside the hours and name it ("Times are in MSK").
  • Allow several ranges a day for breaks; copy a day to others in one step.
  • Preview the next 14 days so the seller sees exactly what customers can book.
  • Say that booked sessions are never changed by saving.

Don't

  • Don't let overlapping or half-filled ranges save; mark both fields and explain.
  • Don't hide the booking window; state how many days ahead customers can book.
  • Don't use 12-hour times without AM or PM; the editor uses 24-hour entry.
