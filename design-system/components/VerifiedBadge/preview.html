Use

  • Name the tier: "Verified seller" or "Verified business".
  • List what was actually checked, with the date.
  • Keep the badge green with a check icon; it is a status, not a decoration.
  • Open the explanation on tap on phones, on hover or focus on desktop.

Don't

  • Show "Not verified" or a grey badge on others.
  • Imply quality: no gold, stars or "Top seller" on this badge.
  • Use orange; verified is success green.
  • Name the checking provider; Ciuna says "checked by Ciuna".
