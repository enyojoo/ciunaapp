Rules

Use
  • Title says what is missing, in plain words. Body gives one reason or reassurance. The action says how to fix it.
  • Errors say whether data is safe and offer retry.
  • No access names the role that can grant it.
Don't
  • Don't use an illustration larger than 56px; orange is for intent, not decoration.
  • Don't show two primary actions.
  • Don't use an empty state while loading; use a skeleton on first load.
