On phones a dialog is a bottom sheet: grabber, full-width stacked buttons with the confirm on top, 48px+ targets. Tapping the scrim or swiping down cancels, except when text has been entered (then ask before discarding). Desktop dialogs are centred, 440px wide, with Cancel left of the confirm.

Rules

Use
  • Title is the direct question: "Reject this order?". Body says what happens to the money, in one or two sentences.
  • Buttons name the action ("Reject order") and the safe option ("Keep order"). Never "OK" or "Yes".
  • Focus lands on the safe action for destructive dialogs. Escape closes. Focus returns to the trigger.
Don't
  • Don't use a dialog for success messages or for anything that Undo could cover.
  • Don't open a dialog from a dialog.
  • Don't make the destructive button the default focus.
