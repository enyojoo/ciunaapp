Rules

Use
  • A sheet is for a short task or a choice that keeps the user in context.
  • Grabber, title and close at the top; the main action in a sticky footer, full width on phones.
  • Trap focus, close on scrim tap and swipe down; ask before discarding entered text.
Don't
  • Don't use a sheet for a multi-step flow; use a page.
  • Don't hide the confirm button below the fold; keep the footer pinned.
  • Don't stack sheets.
