Rules
  • Same frame as every Ciuna email (EmailLayout): logo, one title, one sentence, a label and value block, one button, footer.
  • The subject names the event and the reference. Money states carry no exclamation marks.
  • Times show their zone; the shown zone is the recipient's own, with MSK as the default.
  • A failed transfer leads with "Your money is safe" and gives the refund amount and a time frame.
  • The expired-rate reminder is sent once, when the payment window closes, and never chases the customer after that.
  • No provider names. Payment method is "Bank transfer (SBP)", "SBP QR" or "Pay online".
  • Every email also ships a plain-text part with the same words.
