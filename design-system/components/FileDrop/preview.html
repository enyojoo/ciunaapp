Rules

Use
  • Say the accepted types and size before the user picks: "PDF, JPG or PNG, up to 10 MB".
  • Errors name the file and say what to do next.
  • Phones offer camera and files as two plain buttons; the dashed zone is for desktop drag and drop.
  • Show progress for anything over a second, with a cancel.
Don't
  • Don't reject silently or with "Invalid file".
  • Don't crop images without a preview; images show at 4:3 after upload.
  • Don't make the whole screen a drop target; keep it to the zone.
