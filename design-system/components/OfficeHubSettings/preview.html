4 · Add a method, turn off the default, read-only roles

Add payment method

Enter the name customers will recognise, for example "Bank transfer".
Make this the default for RUB
CancelAdd method

Turn off "Pay by SBP"?

It is the default RUB methodIf you turn it off, "Bank transfer" becomes the default.

Customers who have started an SBP payment can still finish it. New RUB payments will show the other enabled methods.

If this was the only enabled method in a currency, that currency stops accepting payments. Office shows this before you can save.
