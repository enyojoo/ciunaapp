First load only

1No cache, no dataShow a skeleton for the content area. Keep the header, tabs and page title real.
2Cached data existsShow it immediately, refresh in the background. No skeleton, no spinner over it.
3Refreshing or pagingKeep rows on screen; a thin progress hint or a spinner on the button is enough.
4Never over cached contentReplacing visible rows with a skeleton makes the app feel slower than it is.

Rules

Use
  • Match the final layout so nothing jumps when data arrives.
  • Show 3 rows or 4 cards, not a screenful.
  • Shimmer at 1.4s, off under reduced motion. Mark the container aria-busy="true".
Don't
  • Don't skeleton text that is already known (titles, labels, tabs).
  • Don't show a skeleton for more than about 8 seconds: switch to an error with retry.
  • Don't use a full-screen spinner.
