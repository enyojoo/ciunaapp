Counts are pluralised: "1 review", "128 reviews". The built app still shows "1 reviews"

Use

  • Lead with the average in large type, stars below, count under that.
  • Show all five bars even when some are zero.
  • Explain why Write a review is unavailable, in one line.
  • Pluralise the count: "1 review", "128 reviews".

Don't

  • Show a rating for an item with no reviews.
  • Use orange fill in the bars; one primary tone is enough, muted track.
  • Hide the summary when there are reviews but the viewer cannot review.
  • Count reviews hidden by moderation.
