Rules
  • Both lanes are editable. The last edited lane is the source; the other is calculated and shows a dash while the quote loads.
  • Limits come from Office and the customer's verification level; the field never hard-codes them.
  • Errors are one sentence under the figure, with an icon. The quote hides and Continue disables.
  • Flag and code always travel together. Pills are 44px tall.
  • No provider names. Rates are shown with a symbol on each side.
