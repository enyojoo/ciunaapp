Rules

Use
  • One banner per cause; the most severe sits on top.
  • Say what happened and what to do. Name the amount or order.
  • Use a toast for confirmation of an action; use a banner for a state that persists.
Don't
  • Don't stack more than two banners.
  • Don't use orange: it is not a status colour.
  • Don't name payment providers in seller banners.
  • Don't use exclamation marks on money or status.
