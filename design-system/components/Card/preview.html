Rules

Use
  • Surface plate on the paper canvas, hairline border, radius 16, no shadow at rest.
  • Padding 16 on phones, 24 on desktop (the body class gives 20 for dense layouts).
  • Selected = primary-tint + primary-border, plus a check or filled radio so it never relies on colour.
  • An interactive card is one link or button: the whole plate is the target.
Don't
  • Don't nest cards more than once.
  • Don't put two primary buttons in a card.
  • Don't fill a large card with orange.
  • Don't make a card clickable and also put links inside it.
