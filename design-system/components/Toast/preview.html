At most two toasts show at once; the newest sits closest to the nav. A new toast replaces one with the same message. Toasts never cover the primary action: they sit 12px above the nav, and above a sticky action bar when one is present.

Rules

Use
  • Confirm something the user just did: "Order accepted".
  • Offer Undo for removal instead of a confirm dialog.
  • role="status" for success, role="alert" for errors.
Don't
  • Don't use a toast for something the user must act on later; use a banner.
  • Don't put the only copy of an error in a toast; show the error on the field too.
  • Don't say "Success!" or end with an exclamation mark.
