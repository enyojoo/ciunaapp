Spacing scale (4px base)

Page gutter is 20 (16 on small phones). Gap between cards 12 or 16. Section gap 24 to 32. Never use values off the 4px grid.

48px tap target

A 16px icon still gets a 48 × 48 hit area. Rows are at least 56 tall. Controls are 44 tall on web and 48 on touch; space between targets is at least 8.

Focus ring

Accept order Save as draft Delivery

2 to 3px ring in primary (4.56:1 on white, above the 3:1 non-text minimum) with a 2px offset on buttons; inputs get a 3px soft halo and a primary border. Shown with a keyboard or switch, never removed. Do not use the focus ring colour for selected state.
