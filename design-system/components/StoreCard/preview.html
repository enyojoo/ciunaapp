Hidden states

A store that is paused by its seller or suspended by Ciuna is not listed at all: not on the rail, not in search, and the direct link shows "This store is not available right now". Only closed (outside hours) stays visible. Closed stores can be browsed and favourited; Add is disabled on their products.

Use

  • Show the verified icon after the name, and the rating with its count.
  • Say when a closed store opens: "Closed · Opens 9:00", in the viewer's time.
  • Keep closed stores browsable and favouritable.
  • Give delivery fee and ETA, or the free-delivery threshold, on one line.

Don't

  • Hide closed stores; people plan orders ahead.
  • Grey the text of a closed store below AA; only the cover and logo go greyscale.
  • Show "Not verified" on unverified stores.
  • Put the favourite heart anywhere but the cover corner.
