Rules

Use
  • Numbered pagination where people compare or return to rows (tables, Office). Load more on phones.
  • Always show the count: "Showing 1–20 of 248".
  • Current page is filled and has aria-current="page"; arrows are 36px (48 on touch).
  • Keep scroll position when loading more; never jump to the top.
Don't
  • Don't use infinite scroll in tables or anywhere with a footer people need.
  • Don't show more than seven page buttons; collapse with an ellipsis.
  • Don't hide the arrows on the first and last page: dim them.
