Each row becomes one card: identifier and status on top, the key amount and the one row action at the bottom. Sorting moves to a "Sort" menu; selection mode is dropped on phones. Columns that do not fit are dropped in order of least importance, not squeezed.

Rules

Use
  • Left-align text, right-align money and quantities, tabular figures.
  • Status always a badge with a word and icon.
  • The whole row opens the detail; the overflow menu holds secondary actions. Row height 48 Seller, 36 Office.
  • Pagination for tables; "Load more" for phone lists.
Don't
  • Don't scroll a table horizontally on phones.
  • Don't show a skeleton over rows already loaded.
  • Don't mix currencies in one sum column without a symbol on every value.
