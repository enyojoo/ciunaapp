Accessibility rule

A display group is one image: role="img" aria-label="Rated 4.6 out of 5, 128 reviews". The individual star icons are hidden. Input stars are a radiogroup with one radio per value, each named "3 stars". Never convey a rating with colour alone; the number is always in text near the stars.

Use

  • Show the number next to the stars; it carries the decimal.
  • Show the review count on product and store tiles.
  • Make input stars 48px apart so thumbs hit the right one.
  • Name the chosen value in words under the input.

Don't

  • Draw half stars; round the stars and print the number.
  • Show 0.0 for an item with no reviews.
  • Make rating stars orange fill on an orange card.
  • Auto-submit when a star is tapped; the customer still confirms.
