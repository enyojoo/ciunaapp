Rules

Use
  • People are circles, businesses are rounded squares.
  • Tinted primary ground with primary-text initials; the logo orange fill is allowed only for a store's own logo mark.
  • Always provide alt or an accessible name; decorative when beside the name.
Don't
  • Don't use a stock face as a default; show initials.
  • Don't show the verified badge on avatars under 32px.
  • Don't stack more than three.
