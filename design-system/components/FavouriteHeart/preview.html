Use

  • Keep the heart in the same corner on every tile.
  • Update the heart instantly, roll back with a toast if the save fails.
  • Give toggles aria-pressed and a label that names the next action.
  • Show the signed-out prompt as a sheet, not a full page.

Don't

  • Fill a heart orange on a photo it cannot be seen against; keep the white plate.
  • Make the hit area smaller than 48px.
  • Announce saving with a success toast every tap.
