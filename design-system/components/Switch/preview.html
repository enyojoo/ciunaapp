Rules

Use
  • Switch for a setting that takes effect now. The label states the on-state: "Accepting orders".
  • Checkbox for choices that are saved with a form, or for several selections.
  • Radio for one of a few options you want visible; segmented when they are short and switch a view or mode.
  • Whole row is the tap target, at least 48px tall.
Don't
  • Don't use a switch inside a form that needs a Save button.
  • Don't label a switch with a verb ("Enable") or an on/off pair.
  • Don't rely on colour: the thumb position and the on-state label say it.
  • Don't disable without a line saying how to unlock it.
