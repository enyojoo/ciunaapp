# Office

Dense, calm, keyboard-first. The only surface that names providers (YooKassa, Bitbanker).

- **Shell:** grouped sidebar, global search (Cmd-K), a header with the signed-in role. Content on `background`, panels on `card`.
- **Tables:** sticky header, saved views, column chooser, bulk actions, row click opens a side panel, export. Money right-aligned and tabular.
- **Queues:** each queue (merchant applications, listing review, exceptions, payouts, disputes) shows age, owner, SLA and a next action. Items can be claimed.
- **Roles:** `ops`, `support`, `finance`, `compliance`, `super_admin`. A control the signed-in role may not use is hidden, never disabled-without-reason.
- **Audit:** every state change shows who and when; the audit log is append-only.
- **Review layout:** documents and claims on the left, decision and reason on the right, with templated rejection reasons that the merchant sees verbatim.
- **Settings:** split by route (General, Hub services, Payment methods, Rates, Fees, Roles). No single mega-page.
